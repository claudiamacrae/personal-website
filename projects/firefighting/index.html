Fire-fighting Robot

An autonomous robot that navigated a maze, detected a fire, and extinguished it — all without human input.

How It Worked

The robot was powered by an Arduino microcontroller, with all logic and sensor integration written in the Arduino IDE. Ultrasonic sensors allowed it to measure distances to walls and navigate through the maze without collisions. A temperature sensor detected heat signatures to locate the fire. Once the fire was found, a small custom-built oscillating fan kicked in to put it out.

Tech

  • Arduino (microcontroller and code)
  • Ultrasonic sensors (maze navigation)
  • Temperature sensor (fire detection)
  • Custom oscillating fan (fire extinguishing)
